01

Video/Image upload

Kaise kaam karta hai

Video aur image dono ek saath select kar sakte ho, jis order mein select karo usi order mein judenge. Agar audio track attach nahi hai to har image ~5-6s dikhegi (total max 59s). Agar audio attach ho jaaye to yeh limit hat jaati hai — audio ki poori (core) length images mein barabar baant di jaati hai, aur video ko us duration ke hisaab se fast/slow (speed adjust) kar diya jaata hai, taaki audio kabhi bhi cut ya compromise na ho.

    02

    Caption — type karein ya audio se auto

    Kaise kaam karta hai

    Text khud likho, ya English audio (voiceover) attach karo. Exact word sync on ho to caption ka har word voiceover ke saath forced-align hota hai (audio ka har word apni real start/end timing ke saath) — highlight ussi word par jaata hai jo us waqt bola ja raha hai. Text edit karoge to wahi audio se turant dobara align ho jaata hai. Audio attach karne par woh export ki soundtrack ban jaati hai, aur attached video/images us audio ki poori length ke hisaab se adjust ho jaate hain — audio aur video/caption ekdum saath shuru aur saath khatam hote hain.

    Highlight sync 0ms
    Exact sync mein 0ms hi sahi hota hai. Phir bhi agar aapke device par highlight aage/peeche lage to slider se milaiye — right = thoda der se (delay), left = jaldi (early).
    03

    Export

    Media aur text dono chahiye export ke liye.
    Sab browser mein hi process hota hai, koi upload nahi. Export webm/mp4 mein hota hai, minimum 1080p (4:5 = 1080×1350, 9:16 = 1080×1920) aur 12 Mbps bitrate par. Auto-transcribe ek baar Whisper model (~40-70MB) aur Exact word sync ek baar alignment model (~95MB) internet se load karta hai — Claude ke artifact preview mein sandbox ki wajah se kaam nahi karega, isliye yeh file khud host/open karke test karein.
