01

Video/Image upload

Kaise kaam karta hai

Video aur image dono ek saath select kar sakte ho, jis order mein select karo usi order mein judenge. Agar audio track attach nahi hai to har image ~5-6s dikhegi (total max 59s). Agar audio attach ho jaaye to yeh limit hat jaati hai — audio ki poori (core) length images mein barabar baant di jaati hai, aur video ko us duration ke hisaab se fast/slow (speed adjust) kar diya jaata hai, taaki audio kabhi bhi cut ya compromise na ho.

    02

    Caption — type karein ya audio se auto

    Kaise kaam karta hai

    Text khud likho, ya English audio (voiceover) attach karo — transcript + har word ka exact timing auto-generate hoga. Audio attach karne par woh export ki soundtrack ban jaati hai, aur attached video/images us audio ki poori length ke hisaab se automatically adjust ho jaate hain — audio aur video/caption ekdum saath shuru aur saath khatam hote hain, koi extra silent pad nahi.

    Highlight sync 0ms
    Agar highlight voiceover se aage/peeche chal raha hai, is slider se milaiye — right = highlight thoda der se (delay), left = jaldi (early).
    03

    Cover banayein

    4:5
    Kaise kaam karta hai

    Cover us ratio mein banta hai jo upar 4:5 / 9:16 switch mein chuna hai. Background video ka koi bhi frame ho sakta hai, ya apni image. Text wahi bold font mein aata hai jo video caption mein hai, aur dark outline ke saath taaki image par saaf dikhe. Text ko Left ya Center align karo, drag karke kahin bhi rakho, size slider se badlo, chaho to text ke piche apni pasand ke colour ka background lagao, aur colour chun kar kisi word par tap karo (usi colour par dobara tap = wapas white). Cover PNG mein download hota hai — video mein judta nahi.

    04

    Export

    Media aur text dono chahiye export ke liye.
    Sab browser mein hi process hota hai, koi upload nahi. Export webm/mp4 mein hota hai, minimum 1080p (4:5 = 1080×1350, 9:16 = 1080×1920) aur 12 Mbps bitrate par. Auto-transcribe ek baar Whisper model internet se load karta hai (~40-70MB) — Claude ke artifact preview mein sandbox ki wajah se kaam nahi karega, isliye yeh file khud host/open karke test karein.
